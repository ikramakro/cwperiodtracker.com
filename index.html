“Time for your daily check-in”

Why it’s different

Built for the moments a tracker shouldn’t make worse.

Your logs stay on your phone

Guest mode keeps periods, symptoms, and notes on the device. An account is optional and only there if you want sync.

No account to get started

Open the app and tap “Continue as guest.” No email, no sign-up wall before you can log your first day.

Reminders that don’t out you

Lock-screen notifications read “Time for your daily check-in” — never “period in 2 days.” Safer on a shared or borrowed phone.

Never guesses pregnancy

A late cycle is just a late cycle. Pregnancy or postpartum tracking only appears when you choose to turn it on.

Inside the app

Calm, simple cycle tracking.

Track periods, symptoms, mood, and fertility signals. See educational estimates for your next period and fertile window — without alarms, streaks, or pressure.

01

Start as a guest

Skip the account entirely. Your logs live on this phone. If you later want your data on a second device, you can opt in to sync — it’s your call, not a requirement.

02

Today, at a glance

See your cycle day, phase, and an estimated next period. Estimates are educational and labelled as estimates — they’re not a diagnosis, and they’re never a pregnancy guess.

03

A calendar that makes sense

Logged period days, an estimated fertile window, and an estimated ovulation day in one month view. Add symptoms, mood, and fertility signals as you go, and get to know your own rhythm.

04

Discreet by default

Neutral reminder wording is on from the start, and you can preview exactly what appears on your lock screen. Specific wording can reveal more than you meant to — so we avoid it.

05

Privacy controls you can read

AI personalization and analytics are choices you switch on, not defaults you have to hunt for. Our policy says it plainly: we don’t sell your personal information or use health logs for advertising.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Can I download it today?

Not yet. CycleWise is preparing its Google Play release as CW Period & Pregnancy Tracker. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when it’s live.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. We’re starting with Android. If you use an iPhone, pick “iPhone” in the form so we know there’s interest.

Do I need an account?

No. Guest mode keeps your logs on the phone with no account. An optional account exists only if you want to sync between devices.

Is it free?

Core tracking is free and there are no ads. An optional CycleWise Plus upgrade adds advanced insights for people who want them.

Does CycleWise sell my data?

No. Our privacy policy states that we do not sell personal information, do not use health logs for advertising, and do not share menstrual, pregnancy, or sexual-health logs with advertisers or data brokers.

Can I use it for birth control?

No. Fertile-window and period predictions are educational estimates. CycleWise isn’t a medical device or contraception, and it doesn’t replace a clinician.

How do I delete my data?

Guest data lives on your device — uninstalling removes it. If you created an account, you can request deletion in the app. Details are on the delete account page.
