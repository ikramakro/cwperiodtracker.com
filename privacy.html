Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 August 2026 · Android package: com.cwperiodtracker.app

This policy explains how we handle information in the CW Period & Pregnancy Tracker app (also called CycleWise). The app is educational, not a medical device, and is not a substitute for professional care.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the Android app, related cloud APIs used by the app, and optional account features. Guest mode keeps information on the device unless you create an account and choose to sync.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Account data — email, password hash, display name, timezone, locale, and session tokens if you register or sign in.
  • Reproductive health information you enter — period dates, cycle length, symptoms, fertility signals, pregnancy or postpartum details, notes, and related logs. This is health data. We use it only to provide the features you requested.
  • Device data — a device identifier, app version, platform, and optional push token so reminders can be delivered.
  • Consent and settings — privacy choices, notification wording, AI personalization, and analytics opt-in.
  • Support tickets you send through the app.

We do not require a government ID. We only ask for birth year to confirm you are 13 or older. Intimate details are excluded from analytics properties and crash reports.

3. How we use information

  • Provide cycle, pregnancy, and postpartum tracking and educational estimates
  • Sync your data across devices when you are signed in
  • Send check-in reminders you enable (neutral lock-screen wording by default)
  • Optional AI educational tips if you consent
  • Optional redacted product analytics if you consent
  • Security, abuse prevention, and to honor export or deletion requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use health logs for advertising.

4. Where data is stored

Logs can stay on your device. If you create an account, data is sent over HTTPS to our API hosted on a Hostinger VPS. Access tokens are stored in the device’s secure storage. Staff access to intimate records is restricted, time-limited, and audited.

5. Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the service:

  • Hosting — Hostinger (server infrastructure).
  • Push notifications — Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, using a push token. Notification titles and bodies are kept neutral.
  • Legal requests — if required by law.

We do not share menstrual, pregnancy, or sexual health logs with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Retention

Account and synced health data are kept while your account is active. If you request deletion, we schedule a purge after a 30-day grace period, then delete health tables and anonymize remaining account fields. Local data can be removed from the device when you delete the app or complete in-app deletion.

7. Your choices

  • Use guest mode (on-device) without creating an account
  • Turn AI personalization and analytics on or off in Privacy controls
  • Turn reminders off, and keep lock-screen wording neutral
  • Export a copy of your data from the app
  • Request account deletion from the app (30-day grace period)

8. Children

The app is for users 13 years or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, access-controlled APIs, redacted server logs, and device-bound keys for local storage. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

10. International processing

Servers currently run on Hostinger infrastructure. If you use the app from another country, your information may be processed where the servers are located.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, data export, or deletion, use Support in the app (Profile) or the developer contact email shown on the Google Play store listing for CW Period & Pregnancy Tracker.
