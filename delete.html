Delete your account or data

Use this page to request deletion of your CW Period & Pregnancy Tracker account and associated data. Package name: com.cwperiodtracker.app.

Delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open account deletion and confirm.

We schedule a purge after a 30-day grace period. During that window you can cancel in the app. After the grace period, health logs are deleted and remaining account fields are anonymized.

Guest / on-device data

If you used Continue as Guest, data stays on the device. Uninstall the app to remove it.

Delete some data without deleting the account

In the app you can edit or remove daily logs and change privacy controls (AI, analytics, notifications). That does not require deleting the whole account.

Request by email

If you cannot open the app, email the developer contact shown on the Google Play listing for CW Period & Pregnancy Tracker. Include the account email and whether you want full account deletion or a specific data request.

See also the Privacy Policy.
